은/는 vs 이/가 · Which One?

은/는 = the topic: what the sentence is about, known information, contrast ('A is ..., but B is ...'), and self-introductions.

이/가 = the subject as new information: answers to 'who? what?', and with 있어요/없어요, 아니에요, and descriptions of what you notice.

Both can appear in one sentence: 오늘은 (topic) 날씨가 (subject) 좋아요.

Examples

Read, listen if you can, then write each one in the boxes.

저는 학생이에요.
jeoneun haksaengieyo.
I am a student. (about me)
저는학생이에요.
누가 학생이에요? 제가 학생이에요.
nuga haksaengieyo? jega haksaengieyo.
Who is a student? I am.
누가학생이에요?제가학생이에요.
사과는 좋아해요. 배는 안 좋아해요.
sagwaneun joahaeyo. baeneun an joahaeyo.
I like apples. I don't like pears. (contrast)
사과는좋아해요.배는안좋아해요.
밖에 비가 와요.
bakke biga wayo.
It's raining outside. (new information)
밖에비가와요.
오늘은 날씨가 좋아요.
oneureun nalssiga joayo.
Today the weather is nice.
오늘은날씨가좋아요.

Try it: write 은, 는, 이 or 가

  1. 누가 왔어요? 민수＿＿ 왔어요. — Who came? Minsu came.
  2. 저＿＿ 한국 사람이에요. — I am Korean.
  3. 교실에 학생＿＿ 많아요. — There are many students in the classroom.
  4. 이 책＿＿ 재미있지만 저 책은 지루해요. — This book is interesting, but that book is boring.
  5. 방에 고양이＿＿ 있어요. — There is a cat in the room.
  6. 제 이름＿＿ 지민이에요. — My name is Jimin.
Answers

1. 가 · 2. 는 · 3. 이 · 4. 은 · 5. 가 · 6. 은 (이 also OK)
