에게 / 한테 / 께 · To a Person

Marks the person who receives something: give, send, call, write, ask.

에게 = standard (writing and speech). 한테 = casual speech. Both are correct.

께 = honorific, for people you respect: 선생님께, 할머니께, 부모님께, 사장님께.

Examples

Read, listen if you can, then write each one in the boxes.

친구에게 편지를 써요.
chinguege pyeonjireul sseoyo.
I write a letter to a friend.
친구에게편지를써요.
동생한테 선물을 줘요.
dongsaenghante seonmureul jwoyo.
I give a present to my younger sibling.
동생한테선물을줘요.
선생님께 질문해요.
seonsaengnimkke jilmunhaeyo.
I ask the teacher a question.
선생님께질문해요.
엄마한테 전화해요.
eommahante jeonhwahaeyo.
I call my mom.
엄마한테전화해요.
할머니께 꽃을 드려요.
halmeonikke kkocheul deuryeoyo.
I give flowers to Grandma.
할머니께꽃을드려요.

Try it: write 에게/한테 or 께

  1. 친구＿＿ 문자를 보내요. — I send a text to a friend.
  2. 선생님＿＿ 인사해요. — I greet the teacher.
  3. 동생＿＿ 책을 줘요. — I give a book to my younger sibling.
  4. 사장님＿＿ 보고해요. — I report to the boss.
  5. 고양이＿＿ 밥을 줘요. — I feed the cat.
  6. 누구＿＿ 말했어요? — Who did you tell?
Answers

1. 에게 (한테) · 2. 께 · 3. 에게 (한테) · 4. 께 · 5. 에게 (한테) · 6. 에게 (한테)
