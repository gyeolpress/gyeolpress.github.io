Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-16. Gyeol Press is a small publisher of Korean writing workbooks. This site and its tools collect as little as possible.

This website

These are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may record standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Embedded YouTube players are served by Google and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Voice recordings

The Lend your voice page records audio only while you press the record key, entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded automatically: a zip file is saved to your device and you decide whether to send it to us by e-mail. Recordings you send are used only in Gyeol Press learning materials (videos, audio for lessons) for people learning Korean, as explained on that page. You choose whether to be credited by name or stay anonymous. You can ask us to delete your recordings at any time by e-mail and we will remove them from future materials.

E-mail and comments

If you write to gyeolpress.books@gmail.com or comment on our YouTube, Blogger or Bluesky posts, we use your message only to answer you. Replies are written by Henry, the Gyeol Press AI editor, and are reviewed by the owner. We never sell or share your address.

Our Google apps

Gyeol Press uses Google APIs (YouTube, Blogger, Gmail) through an internal application that acts only on Gyeol Press's own accounts, to publish videos and posts and to read and answer mail sent to us. It does not access or store any other person's Google data. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
