Money & Bank · 돈과 은행

Twelve words you will actually use, with romanization. Then three sentences to write.

KoreanRomanizationMeaning
통장tongjangbankbook, account
계좌gyejwabank account
현금hyeongeumcash
동전dongjeoncoin
지폐jipyepaper money
환전hwanjeoncurrency exchange
저금jeogeumsavings
이자ijainterest
세금segeumtax
월세wolsemonthly rent
공짜gongjjafree (of charge)
거스름돈geoseureumdonchange (money)

Use the words

환전을 하고 싶어요.
hwanjeoneul hago sipeoyo.
I'd like to exchange money.
환전을하고싶어요.
거스름돈 여기 있어요.
geoseureumdon yeogi isseoyo.
Here is your change.
거스름돈여기있어요.
월세가 너무 비싸요.
wolsega neomu bissayo.
The rent is too expensive.
월세가너무비싸요.
