Moving Around · 움직임

Twelve words you will actually use, with romanization. Then three sentences to write.

KoreanRomanizationMeaning
타다tadato ride, to get on
내리다naeridato get off
걷다geotdato walk
뛰다ttwidato run
기다리다gidaridato wait
찾다chatdato look for, to find
잃어버리다ireobeoridato lose (something)
묻다mutdato ask
떠나다tteonadato leave
돌아가다doragadato go back
들어가다deureogadato go in
나가다nagadato go out

Use the words

다음 역에서 내려요.
daeum yeogeseo naeryeoyo.
I get off at the next station.
다음역에서내려요.
여기서 기다리세요.
yeogiseo gidariseyo.
Please wait here.
여기서기다리세요.
지갑을 잃어버렸어요.
jigabeul ireobeoryeosseoyo.
I lost my wallet.
지갑을잃어버렸어요.
