Feelings · 감정

Twelve words you will actually use, with romanization. Then three sentences to write.

KoreanRomanizationMeaning
기쁘다gippeudato be glad
슬프다seulpeudato be sad
화나다hwanadato be angry
무섭다museopdato be scary; scared
즐겁다jeulgeopdato be fun, enjoyable
심심하다simsimhadato be bored
외롭다oeropdato be lonely
부끄럽다bukkeureopdato be shy, embarrassed
놀라다nolladato be surprised
걱정geokjeongworry
웃다utdato laugh, to smile
울다uldato cry

Use the words

오늘은 기분이 좋아요.
oneureun gibuni joayo.
I feel good today.
오늘은기분이좋아요.
너무 심심해요.
neomu simsimhaeyo.
I'm so bored.
너무심심해요.
걱정하지 마세요.
geokjeonghaji maseyo.
Don't worry.
걱정하지마세요.
