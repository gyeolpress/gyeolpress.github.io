Describing People · 사람 묘사

Twelve words you will actually use, with romanization. Then three sentences to write.

KoreanRomanizationMeaning
친절하다chinjeolhadato be kind
귀엽다gwiyeopdato be cute
멋있다meositdato be cool, stylish
날씬하다nalssinhadato be slim
조용하다joyonghadato be quiet
활발하다hwalbalhadato be lively, outgoing
부지런하다bujireonhadato be diligent
게으르다geeureudato be lazy
젊다jeomdato be young
늙다neukdato be old (of people)
똑같다ttokgatdato be the same
다르다dareudato be different

Use the words

강아지가 정말 귀여워요.
gangajiga jeongmal gwiyeowoyo.
The puppy is really cute.
강아지가정말귀여워요.
동생은 아주 조용해요.
dongsaengeun aju joyonghaeyo.
My younger sibling is very quiet.
동생은아주조용해요.
우리는 똑같은 옷을 입었어요.
urineun ttokgateun oseul ibeosseoyo.
We wore the same clothes.
우리는똑같은옷을입었어요.
